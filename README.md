# dupe-economy

• Dupe Economy | Full-Stack Product Intelligence Platform
  Product Analyst / Product Strategy Project | May 2026



  Built Dupe Economy, a full-stack AI-powered market intelligence platform that tracks how viral fashion, beauty,
  skincare, and haircare products move through the “dupe economy” from prestige launch to mass-market imitation. The
  system combines product analytics, trend discovery, competitive intelligence, and automated reporting into one
  editorial-style dashboard for analyzing consumer behavior and market response patterns.



  Designed and implemented an end-to-end intelligence pipeline using Next.js, Turso SQLite, Gemini Flash 2.0, and
  Google Search Grounding to identify newly viral products, emerging dupes, brand responses, acquisition signals, price
  gaps, and mass-market copycat behavior. Built a structured database schema to track products, scan runs, findings,
  and market-response signals over time, enabling trend analysis across categories without hardcoding brands or
  competitors.



  Created product-facing dashboards for:



  - Monitoring viral product and dupe activity across fashion, makeup, skincare, and haircare
  - Ranking products by price differential and market disruption potential
  - Tracking weekly scan results and historical intelligence reports
  - Identifying mass-market brands responding fastest to prestige product virality
  - Surfacing early signals around declining trends, acquisitions, brand campaigns, and dupe-led virality



  Applied a product analyst lens to define the platform’s core metrics, data model, workflows, and decision logic,
  turning scattered market signals into actionable intelligence for pricing, competitive positioning, assortment
  strategy, and trend forecasting. The project demonstrates experience in market research automation, data-backed
  product strategy, AI-assisted analysis, full-stack analytics tooling, and consumer trend intelligence.
